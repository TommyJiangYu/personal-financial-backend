# 23: Report personal Expense, cash paid and outstanding balances

**What to build:** Extend reports so users can distinguish their economic Expense from cash paid and can review PAYABLE and RECEIVABLE balances by contact, status and date range.

**Blocked by:** 22: Create split Expenses with PAYABLE or RECEIVABLE.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A split-purchase summary shows personal Expense, cash paid, PAYABLE and RECEIVABLE as separate values.
- [ ] Personal Expense is included on the Expense date regardless of reimbursement status.
- [ ] A case where another person paid shows personal Expense greater than zero and cash paid equal to zero.
- [ ] A case where the owner paid for others shows cash paid greater than personal Expense and a RECEIVABLE balance.
- [ ] Users can list PAYABLE or RECEIVABLE by Financial Contact and date range.
- [ ] Status filters distinguish pending and settled records.
- [ ] Compact list rows show date, category, personal share, cash paid and outstanding amount.
- [ ] Detail view links the Expense, contact alias, Evidence metadata and current reimbursement state.
- [ ] Reports never include a connected user's private reimbursement records.
- [ ] Existing Phase 1.0 non-split reports remain correct when no Reimbursement exists.
